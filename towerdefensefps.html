Protect the Core. Survive the Waves.

Poly Defense is an ambitious solo project combining the strategic planning of Tower Defense with the engaging action of a First-Person Shooter. Set in a Sci-Fi universe involving dimensional travel, players must secure giant Cores while surviving against relentless waves of enemies.

This project has allowed me to dig deep into Unreal Engine 5 to rapidly expand my skill set. From designing intricate AI behaviors using Blackboards and Behavior Trees, to managing complex game loops, economy balancing, and dynamic tower upgrades, I am building every system from the ground up.

Current Development Focus

The "Defense Loop"

I am currently refining the core gameplay loop to ensure both the FPS and TD elements feel equally important. I recently implemented a system where towers are breakable by enemies, forcing the player to actively manage their defenses by repairing them with a hammer mid-combat or in between waves. This creates a tense, dynamic battlefield where enemies calculate whether to target the player or the towers.

AI & Systems Design

A majority of my time has gone into creating intelligent enemy AI using Unreal's Blackboards and Behavior Trees, allowing enemies to make dynamic targeting decisions. I've also implemented a full 3-level upgrade path for each of the tower types (Archer, Wizard, Cannon). In addition, I am working on refining gunplay feedback, including headshot mechanics and clear player/tower health indicators.
