Time to dig deep!

Pick Some Axe is a quirky, fast-paced action platformer built in Unity. Serving as our capstone senior project, the game relies heavily on tight mechanics, a distinct auditory identity, and engaging level environments to bring its subterranean world to life.

Developing this alongside the incredibly talented developers at Chopping Block Studios allowed me to wear many hats. From architecting core player mechanics and designing dynamic levels, to scripting the game's narrative and personally providing the voices for several of our characters, my fingerprints are all over this cave system.

My Contributions

Level Design: The Grove & The Mantle

Working closely with a teammate, we co-created the game's major zones: The Grove and The Mantle. My primary focus was dedicated to bringing The Grove to life. I was responsible for implementing the core functionality that made the space interactive, specifically programming the movable minecarts and the illuminating light crystal networks that players must navigate and utilize to progress.

Systems: The Belly Bash

I was responsible for developing several core player systems, most notably creating the Belly Bash mechanic. This required extensive iteration—in fact, it originally started as a "Belly Flop" mechanic! Through playtesting and refinement, I adapted the system into the punchier, more satisfying Belly Bash that shipped in the final build.

Sound Design & VO

I created the sounds for a majority of the game! Beyond producing the SFX—including the game's iconic, highly satisfying Gembit pickup sound—I also stepped into the recording booth. I provided the official character Voice Over for Joe, Coalbert, and Ironson, giving each character their distinct personality.

Narrative & UI Implementation

To tie the experience together, I created and continually iterated on the simple, charming narrative of Pick Some Axe, ensuring the story paced well with the gameplay. On the front-end, I also took on UI responsibilities, including designing and programming the final Credits screen to properly celebrate the team's hard work.
