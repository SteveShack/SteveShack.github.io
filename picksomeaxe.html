Ready to chop your way to the top?

Pick Some Axe is a high-energy action-platformer where precision movement and heavy-hitting combat are the keys to survival. Built in Unity as our capstone senior game leading up to our May 2026 graduation, this project required tight mechanics and a highly coordinated development pipeline.

I had the incredible opportunity to collaborate with the team at Chopping Block Studios. Working alongside Dylan on design, Ryan in programming, Elara keeping us on track in production, and our incredibly talented art team, we built an experience designed to be frantic, fun, and visually striking.
