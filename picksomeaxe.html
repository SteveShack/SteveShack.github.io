Time to dig deep!

In Pick Some Axe, you step into the boots of Joe Miner, embarking on a quest to rescue your beloved husband, Jasper, from depths no spelunker dares to go. To survive, you must navigate treacherous terrains, battle aggressive Pebblets, and master innovative mechanics like digging and the dynamic Belly Bash.

Developing this capstone senior project allowed me to wear many hats. From architecting core player systems and building out the lush environments of The Grove, to scripting the game's narrative and personally recording character voices, my work spanned the entire development pipeline.

My Contributions

Level Design: The Grove

Working closely with a teammate to co-create The Grove and The Mantle, I poured a massive share of my effort into seeing The Grove come to life. I was responsible for the core interactive elements of the level, programming the functionality for the movable minecarts and the illuminating light crystals that guide players through the lush, overgrown caverns.

Systems: The Belly Bash & Sulfric

Early into the project I developed the Belly Bash mechanic. This was the first of Joe's Belly-related moves to get implemented! Concepted with the idea of having Joe throw himself belly-first into enemies, it allowed us to utilize his rotund stature in interesting ways which, when combined with the digging mechanic, allows for all sorts of traversal and attacks.

This mechanic truly shines when interacting with Sulfric, a new explosive enemy introduced in this level. The entire combat loop here revolves around using the Belly Bash to forcefully knock Sulfric into other enemies, triggering satisfying chain-reaction explosions to clear the room!

Sound Design & VO

I created the sound effects for a majority of the game! A few of these sounds include the iconic and incredibly satisfying Gembit pickup sound. In addition to sound design, I also provided the official character Voice Over for Joe Miner, Coalbert, and Ironson, bringing our stylized cast of characters to life!

Narrative & UI

To tie the subterranean adventure together, I created and continually iterated on the simple narrative of Pick Some Axe, ensuring the story paced well with the gameplay loop. On the front-end, I also handled UI responsibilities, designing and programming elements like the game's final Credits screen to properly celebrate our team's hard work.
