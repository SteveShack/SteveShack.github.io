POOL

Land On Our Pool

GMTK Jam 2025 Unity Audio Design
Play on Itch.io

The Concept

Created during the four-day GMTK 2025 Game Jam, our team was tasked with the theme LOOP. During our brainstorming phase, we decided to literally reverse the word to get POOL. From there, the concept was born: a simple, highly-replayable game where you must successfully land in a pool over and over again from increasingly terrifying heights.

We added the subtitle "Land On Our Pool" as a fun way to sneak the word LOOP back into the title. If you can manage to dive down from the absolute highest point, you win!

My Contributions
  • Audio & Music: I took on the responsibility of creating the sound effects and music for the game. This was a fantastic learning experience and served as direct preparation for my audio work on our larger project, Pick Some Axe.
  • Unity & Ragdolls: The jam was a great excuse to get hands-on with Unity's physics and experiment heavily with its ragdoll system, creating the hilarious and chaotic falling mechanics you see in the game.
  • Team Leadership: Beyond the engine, I focused on scheduling and leading the team. The goal wasn't just to make a game, but to bond as a unit before tackling larger studio projects. Coordinating a remote jam team is tough, but incredibly rewarding!
